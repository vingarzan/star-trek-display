SOVEREIGN WALL MOUNT — REVISION 2 PRINTABLE PROTOTYPES
Units: millimetres. Print at 100% scale.

A_hex_carrier_244mm.stl: 244 x 215.96 x 8 mm. Enlarges the supplied hexagon frame and edge connections in X/Y only, adds a reinforced centre, four captive M4 nut pockets and two wall fixing holes. Fixing centres are 156 mm apart, with 4.6 mm through holes and 9.5 mm diameter front head recesses. Use suitable wall fixings; this is not a rated nail-only mount. Edge connector fit still requires a physical check.

B_socket_arm_5deg_up.stl: 80 x 80 x 60.67 mm. Reinforced arm with a 10.4 mm square socket and 3.4 mm cross-bolt bore. The socket axis leans 5 degrees toward +Y (up the wall).

peg_insert_1.stl and peg_insert_2.stl: two IDENTICAL interchangeable inserts, each 18 x 18 x 33.6 mm. Each has a 10 mm square stem, collar, and 7.8 mm diameter round peg projecting 19 mm from the collar for the measured 8.1 mm ship hole. Orthogonal 3.4 mm cross-bores allow 90-degree indexing. Glue ONLY the round peg into the ship. Never glue the square stem into the arm. Use one insert per ship and keep the second as a spare or for another ship.

A removable M3 cross-bolt with washers and a locking nut retains the insert. The receiver is 20 mm across: an M3 x 30 mm bolt is a nominal starting length, subject to actual washer/nut dimensions and physical fit. Insert from either side. Tighten only enough to secure the nut; do not crush the printed receiver.

To reorient or move a ship: support its weight, remove the cross-bolt, lift the ship and its glued insert out, rotate the square stem 90 degrees, reinsert, and reinstall the bolt. Keep the arm itself pointing upward. The ship's first glued position must be chosen so these 90-degree steps correspond to nose-up and sideways. No adhesive needs to be released.

B_arm_and_two_inserts_layout.stl is a convenient three-part print layout, not an assembled model. Print it OR the three individual files, not both.

The larger hexagons mate with copies of this 244 mm version. Their scaled connectors and changed spacing are NOT compatible with the original 210 mm hexagons.
Print envelope: all new components fit within the user's 250 x 250 mm limit. The existing 254.10 x 244.82 mm decorative base is reused, not reprinted.

FIT FIRST
Print the 7.7, 7.8 and 7.9 mm peg coupons using the intended material and settings. All have a 19 mm exposed peg. Check insertion by hand without forcing or glue. The inserts currently use 7.8 mm. A different coupon result requires changing the inserts. Also print socket_fit_test.stl and one insert: check the square sliding fit, collar seating and M3 cross-bolt alignment at both 0 and 90 degrees before printing the full arm. The glue clearance and compatibility must match the actual ship material and chosen adhesive.

DRILLING AND ASSEMBLY
1. Remove the original stand arm and flatten its remaining stump. Preserve the decorative base. The digital illustration clips at the source base's maximum height of 11.4 mm; actual cutting must follow the local base surface.
2. Dry-position the 80 mm arm flange on the reused base. Its four 4.6 mm bolt holes form a 56 x 56 mm square. The supplied 2 mm thick template provides 2.5 mm pilot guides and a centre hole. Confirm every proposed hole lies in sound material and the flange can sit evenly before drilling.
3. The illustrated flange centre corresponds to original STL X=133.912, Y=101.999. This is a positioning reference, not a mandatory physical measurement. Raised decorations may need local levelling or rigid spacers. Do not pull a rocking flange flat by overtightening.
4. Load four M4 nuts into the rear hexagonal pockets of A before mounting it. Check fit; nut pockets are about 7.40 mm across flats, 3.4 mm deep.
5. Secure A independently to the wall at both fixing points, with +Y upward. The existing hex edge connectors provide alignment; their ability to carry a 600 g ship has not been established.
6. Sandwich the decorative base between A and B using four M4 machine screws and suitable washers. Select lengths from the actual stack thickness, including any levelling spacers: achieve full nut engagement without protruding behind A into the wall. No single screw length is specified because the decorative base thickness varies.
7. Check ship clearance in the desired orientation before applying adhesive. Glue fixes the ship to its insert; the square socket permits subsequent 90-degree reorientation. Follow the adhesive's compatibility and full-cure instructions, supporting the ship during curing.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
All individual part STLs are watertight, positive-volume, single connected solids. The combined arm-and-inserts layout intentionally contains three separate parts. See geometry_checks.json. These are geometry checks, not a structural rating. No physical print, slicer inspection, glue-joint test or 600 g load test has been performed. Check the assembled mount over a padded surface before installing a ship. The cross-bolt mechanically retains the insert in the arm; the ship-to-peg connection still relies on the glue joint. The peg/socket assembled geometry was checked for unintended overlap; physical tolerances remain unverified.

The original base overhangs one hexagon and will hide much of it. Adjacent ship/base clearance may require empty hexagons between loaded mounts. No ship STL was supplied, so hull/shoulder contact, wall clearance, centre of gravity and neighbouring ship interference are unverified. No printer profile or G-code is included.
