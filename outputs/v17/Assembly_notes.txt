CURRENT DESIGN
hex_open_original_size.stl is ONLY the original outside contour, edge connectors and original nail tab. The fighter-plane upright and its diagonal supporting arm are removed completely. There is no central plate, crossbar or fixing spine.
hex_plaque_flush_clips.stl uses that same minimal frame, adding only two lower cradle clips integrated with its bottom edge. No centre plate, rear stand-offs, upper arm, latch or keeper remains. No plaque drilling or adhesive is required.
hex_ship_integrated_rounded_arm.stl combines the structural hexagon and B dual-socket arm into one continuous printable solid. It replaces both hex_ship_original_size.stl and B_dual_socket_arm_130mm.stl. No arm-to-frame screws, nuts or assembly are needed. The four old attachment holes are filled. The base is now a compact 8 mm thick junction whose 60-degree edges extend into the horizontal and vertical ribs, without the previous short horizontal base edges and flat faces, replacing the former 80 x 80 x 16 mm rounded pad. Its thickness matches the hexagon. The beam and diagonal brace extend back into this thinner junction, preserving a continuous solid connection. Beam and brace edges retain 3 mm rounding; the end receiver retains 2.5 mm rounding. Functional socket openings and cross-bolt bores retain their dimensions.
All three frames are 210 x 185.865 mm in plan, matching the original fighter-display size. The open frame is 8 mm thick. The integrated ship mount is 166.773 mm deep overall, with an 8 mm outer frame. The plaque holder is 17.3 mm deep including the retaining lips.

PLAQUE POSITION, CLIPS AND UPPER-RIGHT LATCH
The plaque remains vertical, single point up, and flush against the 8 mm frame face. It sits 2.607 mm lower than revision 12 (reference Y=-5.107 mm). No drilling of the plaque is required.
The lower clip seats are now 6 mm wide, centred at X=-48 and +48 mm near the lower hex corners. Their front retaining lips are 4.2 mm wide, relieved at the outer side to avoid the raised emblem. Seats remain at Y=-87.43 mm. The nominal rim gap remains 6.3 mm for a 5.7 mm rim.
A separate rounded upper-right latch fits at X=51, Y=88.3 mm. It includes its own spacer foot and uses one M3 x 16 screw through a 3.4 mm clearance hole into a 2.6 mm pilot hole in the frame. Carefully form or tap the thread and tighten lightly; screw engagement and durability need a physical trial. Do not force an oversized screw or overtighten the small printed part.
All printed clip and installed latch material stays inside the original hex outline. This is a removable keeper, not a snap latch or captive swivel. There is no rear plaque stand-off.

INSTALL / REMOVE PLAQUE
1. Mount the frame and remove the latch and its screw.
2. Lower the plaque into the two lower clips, against the frame, single emblem point upward.
3. Place the latch foot onto the upper-right frame corner, with its rounded tongue down over the plaque rim. Install the M3 x 16 screw gently. The tongue retains the plaque without clamping its decorated face.
4. To remove, unscrew and lift the entire latch straight forward, then lift the plaque about 10 mm out of the lower clips. Do not try to lift the plaque while the latch spacer remains installed.
Print the supplied rim gap sample first. Check the actual clip contact and latch on the decorative plaque before wall use; geometric clearance does not establish retention strength. The latch is a small separately printed part; inspect its print orientation and support needs in the slicer.

INTEGRATED SHIP ARM
Print hex_ship_integrated_rounded_arm.stl as one part. The extended root meets the frame at the same 8 mm thickness. Added-material rounded collars replace the angular chamfers at the arm root and brace foot. The main collar uses a 6 mm circular fillet profile, tangent to the base face and the arm wall, with rounded corners around its perimeter. It spreads 6 mm beyond the main arm faces at the base and joins the arm 6 mm above the frame. The base outline and flat underside remain unrounded. The two peg inserts remain removable and still use M3 cross-bolts.
The top peg axis is 130 mm beyond the ship hex front, approximately 138 mm from the wall. Use the upward socket for level flight with the nose sideways along the wall. Use the end socket, tilted 5 degrees upward, for the other display plane. The same removable insert fits both.
Glue only the round 7.8 mm diameter x 19 mm exposed peg into the ship, not the square stem into the arm. The two inserts are identical. Support the ship, remove the M3 cross-bolt, withdraw the insert, choose its socket and 90-degree index, and reinstall the bolt. No reorientation under load. Verify cross-bolt length, washers/nut engagement and hull access using the actual hardware. The glue-to-ship joint still relies on adhesive compatibility and full cure.
