PLAQUE POSITION AND FIT
The decorative base remains upright: its single point is up, and its two other points are down. The orientation is the prior 125.044-degree rotation from the source STL. It remains raised 22 mm relative to revision 7, with reference offset Y=+2 mm from the hex centre.
The plaque back now contacts the hexagon's front surface directly at Z=8 mm. The former 4.25 mm stand-off is removed. This means flush AGAINST the hexagon, not flush with the wall behind the 8 mm frame.
Two lower clips at X=-35 and +35 mm support the plain disc rim. Their bottoms are about 0.524 mm above the hexagon's lower line, and all new clip material stays inside the regular hex outline. The decorative emblem itself is larger than the frame and still protrudes beyond it.
The rim is 5.7 mm thick in the two contact areas. Each clip has a 6.3 mm slot: 0.6 mm nominal front clearance. The rear side is the existing frame surface. The only raised parts are the seat/front lips necessary to hold the disc edge.
No latch is included, as requested. This is a lift-out, gravity-seated cradle. Check that the real plaque rests evenly and stays upright without unwanted turning or rocking.

INSTALL THE PLAQUE
1. Remove the original table-stand upright, flush with its local decorative surface. Do not cut the outer disc or emblem tips.
2. Fix the plaque hexagon to the wall using its original nail tab before fitting the disc. The disc may cover access to the fixing.
3. Hold the upright plaque about 10 mm above its final position.
4. Move it toward the wall until its back is against the frame face.
5. Lower it into both cradles, with the disc behind the retaining lips. Let both seats support it; do not rest an emblem tip on a clip.
To remove, support the plaque, lift it about 10 mm and bring it forward. No fastener or latch needs releasing. Do not force the rigid clips to flex.
Print rim_clip_fit_test.stl first in the intended material, and test it on both plain rim contact areas. It checks the same 6.3 mm gap; its standalone back thickness does not represent a stand-off in the final mount. Adjust tolerances if needed rather than globally scaling the frame. Clean any support material or first-layer expansion from mating/contact surfaces.
