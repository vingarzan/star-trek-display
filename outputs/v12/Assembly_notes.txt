PLAQUE POSITION AND FIT
The decorative base remains upright: its single point is up, and its two other points are down. The orientation is the prior 125.044-degree rotation from the source STL. It sits 4.5 mm lower than revision 11 to meet the relocated corner clips, with reference offset Y=-2.5 mm from the hex centre.
The plaque back now contacts the hexagon's front surface directly at Z=8 mm. The former 4.25 mm stand-off is removed. This means flush AGAINST the hexagon, not flush with the wall behind the 8 mm frame.
Two 12 mm wide clips at X=-45.5 and +45.5 mm sit just inside the two lower hexagon corners, with outer edges 1 mm inward from the corner vertices. Their bottoms are about 0.502 mm above the bottom line. The retaining lips rise 3 mm above the seats to clear the raised emblem. All clip material stays inside the regular hex outline. The decorative emblem itself is larger than the frame and still protrudes beyond it.
The rim is 5.7 mm thick in the two contact areas. Each clip has a 6.3 mm slot: 0.6 mm nominal front clearance. The rear side is the existing frame surface. The only raised parts are the seat/front lips necessary to hold the disc edge.
No latch is included, as requested. This is a lift-out, gravity-seated cradle. Check that the real plaque rests evenly and stays upright without unwanted turning or rocking.

INSTALL THE PLAQUE
1. Remove the original table-stand upright, flush with its local decorative surface. Do not cut the outer disc or emblem tips.
2. Fix the plaque hexagon to the wall using its original nail tab before fitting the disc. The disc may cover access to the fixing.
3. Hold the upright plaque about 10 mm above its final position.
4. Move it toward the wall until its back is against the frame face.
5. Lower it into both cradles, with the disc behind the retaining lips. Let both seats support it; do not rest an emblem tip on a clip.
To remove, support the plaque, lift it about 10 mm and bring it forward. No fastener or latch needs releasing. Do not force the rigid clips to flex.
Print rim_clip_fit_test.stl first in the intended material, and test it on both plain rim contact areas. It checks the same 6.3 mm gap; its standalone back thickness does not represent a stand-off in the final mount. Adjust tolerances if needed rather than globally scaling the frame. Clean any support material or first-layer expansion from mating/contact surfaces.

INTEGRATED SHIP ARM
Print hex_ship_integrated_rounded_arm.stl as one part. The rounded root spans the former frame/flange joint without an attachment interface. The two peg inserts remain removable and still use M3 cross-bolts.
The top peg axis is 130 mm beyond the ship hex front, approximately 138 mm from the wall. Use the upward socket for level flight with the nose sideways along the wall. Use the end socket, tilted 5 degrees upward, for the other display plane. The same removable insert fits both.
Glue only the round 7.8 mm diameter x 19 mm exposed peg into the ship, not the square stem into the arm. The two inserts are identical. Support the ship, remove the M3 cross-bolt, withdraw the insert, choose its socket and 90-degree index, and reinstall the bolt. No reorientation under load. Verify cross-bolt length, washers/nut engagement and hull access using the actual hardware. The glue-to-ship joint still relies on adhesive compatibility and full cure.
