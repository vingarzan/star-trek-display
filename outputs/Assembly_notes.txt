FIT FIRST
Print the 7.7, 7.8 and 7.9 mm peg coupons using the intended material and settings. All have a 19 mm exposed peg. Check insertion by hand without forcing or glue. B currently uses 7.8 mm. A different coupon result requires changing B before printing it. The glue clearance and compatibility must match the actual ship material and chosen adhesive.

DRILLING AND ASSEMBLY
1. Remove the original stand arm and flatten its remaining stump. Preserve the decorative base. The digital illustration clips at the source base's maximum height of 11.4 mm; actual cutting must follow the local base surface.
2. Dry-position the 80 mm arm flange on the reused base. Its four 4.6 mm bolt holes form a 56 x 56 mm square. The supplied 2 mm thick template provides 2.5 mm pilot guides and a centre hole. Confirm every proposed hole lies in sound material and the flange can sit evenly before drilling.
3. The illustrated flange centre corresponds to original STL X=133.912, Y=101.999. This is a positioning reference, not a mandatory physical measurement. Raised decorations may need local levelling or rigid spacers. Do not pull a rocking flange flat by overtightening.
4. Load four M4 nuts into the rear hexagonal pockets of A before mounting it. Check fit; nut pockets are about 7.40 mm across flats, 3.4 mm deep.
5. Secure A independently to the wall at both fixing points, with +Y upward. The existing hex edge connectors provide alignment; their ability to carry a 600 g ship has not been established.
6. Sandwich the decorative base between A and B using four M4 machine screws and suitable washers. Select lengths from the actual stack thickness, including any levelling spacers: achieve full nut engagement without protruding behind A into the wall. No single screw length is specified because the decorative base thickness varies.
7. Check ship clearance in the desired orientation before applying adhesive. Glue fixes that chosen orientation. Follow the adhesive's compatibility and full-cure instructions, supporting the ship during curing.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
Both primary STLs are watertight, positive-volume, single connected solids. See geometry_checks.json. These are geometry checks, not a structural rating. No physical print, slicer inspection, glue-joint test or 600 g load test has been performed. Check the assembled mount over a padded surface before installing a ship. The angled peg alone is not a mechanical lock.

The original base overhangs one hexagon and will hide much of it. Adjacent ship/base clearance may require empty hexagons between loaded mounts. No ship STL was supplied, so hull/shoulder contact, wall clearance, centre of gravity and neighbouring ship interference are unverified. No printer profile or G-code is included.
