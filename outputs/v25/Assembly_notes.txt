INTEGRATED SHIP MOUNT
hex_ship_integrated_rounded_arm.stl is one piece. Its central base is a regular hexagon, aligned with the original outer frame and shifted 8.2 mm downward: 74 mm wide, 64.086 mm tall and 8 mm thick. All six base edges are parallel to corresponding outer frame edges. The flat underside and unrounded base outline are retained.
Two 12 x 8 mm diagonal cross-braces meet the four sloping frame sides perpendicularly. The vertical brace is now 12 x 8 mm, matching both diagonal braces. All three frame braces are twice the 6 mm outer border width. The local nail-cap housing remains 18 mm wide to accommodate the recessed seat and cap. The nail seat is recessed again: 5 mm above the back, 3 mm below the front of the frame. The 14 x 12 mm pocket retains solid walls and a continuous connection to the vertical brace. The nail hole position and outline are unchanged.
A removable hollow cap hides the head. It stands 2.4 mm above the frame when nominally seated and provides clearance for a centred head up to 9 mm diameter and 4 mm height above the seat. Check your actual hardware before use.
Print nail_cover_cap.stl in its supplied orientation, flat roof down and hollow side up. Print fit_test_nail_cover.stl first and test the cap on it. Two slotted friction fingers have nominal 0.05 mm interference per side; retention and durability depend on printer and material. Do not force a tight fit. The cap is cosmetic and carries no ship load.
After mounting, press the cap gently into the pocket. Use the small edge notch to lift it off for fastener access. No adhesive is needed.
Rounded added-material transitions support the arm root, with the lower transition following the angled strengthening brace. The mount requires no arm-to-frame bolts.
The upward socket axis is 130 mm beyond the frame front, 138 mm from the wall. Both removable peg inserts and M3 cross-bolt holes retain their previous positions. The end socket points outward and 5 degrees upward. Use the upward socket for belly-horizontal flight with the nose sideways.

PLAQUE POSITION, CLIPS AND UPPER-RIGHT LATCH
The plaque remains vertical, single point up, and flush against the 8 mm frame face. It sits 2.607 mm lower than revision 12 (reference Y=-5.107 mm). No drilling of the plaque is required.
The lower clip seats are now 6 mm wide, centred at X=-48 and +48 mm near the lower hex corners. Their front retaining lips are 4.2 mm wide, relieved at the outer side to avoid the raised emblem. Seats remain at Y=-87.43 mm. The nominal rim gap remains 6.3 mm for a 5.7 mm rim.
A separate rounded upper-right latch fits at X=51, Y=88.3 mm. It includes its own spacer foot and uses one M3 x 16 screw through a 3.4 mm clearance hole into a 2.6 mm pilot hole in the frame. Carefully form or tap the thread and tighten lightly; screw engagement and durability need a physical trial. Do not force an oversized screw or overtighten the small printed part.
All printed clip and installed latch material stays inside the original hex outline. This is a removable keeper, not a snap latch or captive swivel. There is no rear plaque stand-off.

INSTALL / REMOVE PLAQUE
1. Mount the frame and remove the latch and its screw.
2. Lower the plaque into the two lower clips, against the frame, single emblem point upward.
3. Place the latch foot onto the upper-right frame corner, with its rounded tongue down over the plaque rim. Install the M3 x 16 screw gently. The tongue retains the plaque without clamping its decorated face.
4. To remove, unscrew and lift the entire latch straight forward, then lift the plaque about 10 mm out of the lower clips. Do not try to lift the plaque while the latch spacer remains installed.
Print the supplied rim gap sample first. Check the actual clip contact and latch on the decorative plaque before wall use; geometric clearance does not establish retention strength. The latch is a small separately printed part; inspect its print orientation and support needs in the slicer.

PRINTING AND CHECKS
The complete mount fits within 250 x 250 mm XY at approximately 166.8 mm height. No validated print profile or G-code is supplied. Inspect support access, orientation and layer direction in the slicer. Print peg, rim and original connector samples before full parts.

These checks do not establish physical strength, long-term creep resistance or wall-fixing capacity. Test physical fit and the finished mount before using it with a ship.
